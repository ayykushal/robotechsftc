Our content

We share team news, engineering work, and educational resources. You may link to these public pages. Learning and building safely

Tutorials describe our team’s experiences and may need to be adapted to your equipment. Check current competition rules and manufacturer instructions. Use suitable supervision and safety precautions when building, using tools, or operating a robot.

Accuracy and availability

We aim to keep these pages useful, but designs, event details, and external resources can change. Confirm important dates and requirements with the relevant organiser. This site does not guarantee competition results or uninterrupted access to external services.

Respectful use

Do not misuse contact details, send spam, attempt unauthorised access, or interfere with the website. Contact messages should relate to us or our activities.

Links and contact

External sites set their own terms and privacy practices. The contact form opens a Gmail draft; a message is only sent when you review it and press Send in Gmail. The form does not create an event registration, sponsorship commitment, or purchase.

Updates

We may update these terms as the site changes. Questions can go to our email above.
