Hands-on STEM

At RoboFest, library workshops, coding sessions, and Hallie Wells Middle School’s STEAM Night, we introduce students and families to FIRST and robotics. We make room for people to explore, ask questions, and try something new.

Explore our events

Mentoring new teams

We support FLL teams with robot design, coding, and Innovation Project ideas, including helping a rookie team move from FLL Explore to FLL Challenge. Our shared CAD files, code resources, and tutorials help other teams keep learning.

Open our shared resources

Stronger together

With other FIRST teams in the MoCo Robotics Alliance, we advocate for more access to STEM opportunities in Montgomery County. Our community work also includes Chesapeake Bay cleanup and fundraising initiatives.
