2025 Richmond, VA Qualifier I

December 13, 2025 · Falling Creek Middle School, Richmond, Virginia

Past event

Event details

December 13, 2025 · 07:00 AM – 06:00 PM Eastern Time

Falling Creek Middle School

Our first qualifier of the 2025–26 season took us to Richmond, Virginia, where we met new teams and competed in DECODE matches. We earned first place for the Reach Award, a milestone we were proud to bring home. Although we did not advance, the experience gave us a strong start to the season and motivation to keep improving and making connections.
