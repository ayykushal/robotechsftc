Event details

October 25, 2025 · 02:00 PM – 05:00 PM Eastern Time

Snowden Farm ES

On October 25, we hosted RoboFest at Snowden Farm Elementary School to share STEM with our community. Four FTC teams joined the event, with interactive stations featuring live robot demonstrations, building challenges, and engineering showcases. Visitors explored robots, tried hands-on activities, and learned about science and technology in a fun, relaxed setting.
